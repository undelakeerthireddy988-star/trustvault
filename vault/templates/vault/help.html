❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I upload a document?

Go to Dashboard → In "Add a document" write the name → Choose file → Click "Save to my vault". Done!

2. How do I view / download a document?

In My Documents list, click "View" next to your document. It will open in a new tab.

3. How do I delete a document?

Click "Delete" next to the document → Confirm it. It will be deleted permanently.

4. I cannot find my document, what to do?

Search by name in "Find your documents" above. Or use All / Documents / Images / Others filter.

5. Which files can I upload?

PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, TXT - everything works. Aadhaar, PAN, Marksheet, Certificate - anything.

6. I forgot my password, what to do?

Forgot Password feature is not available yet. Please contact admin or create a new account.

7. Are my documents safe?

Yes, only you can see your documents after login. Another user cannot see your documents.
