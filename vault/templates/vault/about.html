About TrustVault

Your documents. Your vault. Your secret.

What is TrustVault?

TrustVault is a secure personal document vault. You can store your important documents like Aadhaar, PAN Card, Marksheets, Certificates, and Photos in one safe place. Login from anywhere, anytime and access your documents.

✨ Features

  • 🔒 Secure Login - Only your account, only your documents
  • 📁 Easy Upload - Upload any file - PDF, Image, Doc
  • 🔍 Smart Search - Find any document instantly by name
  • 🗂️ Filter - View separately in Documents, Images, Others
  • 👤 My Profile - Manage your details in one place

🚀 How it works?

1. Sign Up   →   2. Login   →   3. Upload Document   →   4. View / Download whenever you need. That's it!
